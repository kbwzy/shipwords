# AI Pose Gallery design direction

- **Audience**: illustrators, photographers and image creators looking for clear pose references.
- **Mood**: a dark photographic studio stage — warm near-black coffee tones with a single soft spotlight — where cream paper panels flip the contrast for content. Editorial, tactile, confident.
- **Fonts**: a high-contrast serif display (Georgia/Palatino stack, italic emphasis) over a clean sans body; `ui-monospace` for micro-labels (REC-style tags, section eyebrows).
- **Palette**: stage `#14100d`, panel `#1c1613`, cream `#f2ead9`, ink `#201812`, amber accent `#e8a04c` used sparingly, REC red `#e5483a` only for the live dot, muted `#a39684`, hairline `rgba(242,234,217,.12)`.
- **Depth**: cards and panels sit on the stage with a 1px hairline plus one large soft shadow (`0 40px 90px rgba(0,0,0,.55)`); the amber glow appears only on the primary action. No gradients as decoration.
- **First screen**: dark hero with an oversized cream serif headline, mono eyebrow with REC dot, tilted reference cards right, and the studio CTA glowing amber.
- **Workspace**: a cream paper panel on the dark stage (tinydeskai FAQ-card flip), form left / preview right, amber primary button, hairline dividers.
- **Gallery**: dense grid like a contact sheet — numbered ID badges on each card, count-bearing category chips, hover reveals a "Use this pose" affordance, hairline separators, mono meta row.
- **Interaction**: category filters, quick search, a keyboard-accessible detail view, prompt copying and individual PNG downloads. Free trial gating and copy stay as built.
- **Assets**: original AI-generated mannequin and figure studies, clearly identified; no third-party reference archive.
- **Avoid**: generic gradient SaaS heroes, neon cyber palettes, pure-white flash panels, exaggerated counts and unverified generation claims.
